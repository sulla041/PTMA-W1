Integrated circuits are naturally abstracted as graphs, yet conventional graph neural networks struggle to align individual logic gates and multi-dimensional physical interconnections within a unified representational space. Existing methods compress continuous edge parasitics into scalar weights and suffer from over-smoothing across deep logic paths. To address these challenges, we propose an end-to-end point-set graph dynamics framework that projects heterogeneous nodes and complete relation vectors into a unified latent space. By reformulating graph modeling as computing structural displacements over a point set, continuous relation attributes directly drive pairwise interaction fields and competitive acquisition coefficients, supporting deep propagation without feature collapse. Furthermore, hierarchical virtual meter networks unify macroscopic whole-graph prediction into the same point-set readout interface. Extensive experiments on CircuitNet-3 and DeepGate2 benchmarks validate the effectiveness and generality of our methodology.
