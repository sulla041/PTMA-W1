Integrated circuits are naturally abstracted as graphs, making graph modeling a prominent paradigm for physical and timing prediction. However, conventional graph neural networks fundamentally rely on message passing that suffers from over-smoothing across deep multi-hop paths, while existing approaches compress multi-dimensional interconnections into scalar weights, losing fine-grained physical parasitics and load characteristics. To address these challenges, we propose an end-to-end point-set graph dynamics framework that maps the circuit graph into a point set and learns structural offsets through pairwise interaction patterns and competitive acquisition coefficients. By computing all-directional velocity displacements rather than spatial averages, our dynamics supports deep multi-hop propagation without suffering from feature collapse, while allowing complete multi-dimensional relation vectors to directly modulate state updates. Furthermore, hierarchical virtual meter networks unify macroscopic whole-graph metric extraction into the same point-set readout interface. Extensive experiments across diverse downstream tasks on open-source CircuitNet-3 and DeepGate benchmarks validate the effectiveness and generality of our methodology.
