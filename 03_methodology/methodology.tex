\begin{figure*}[t]

  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/method01.pdf}
  \caption{Overall framework of the proposed point-set graph dynamics. Stage 1: The circuit netlist is formulated as an attributed directed graph preserving heterogeneous gate features and continuous relation attributes. Stage 2: Entity nodes and multi-dimensional relations are projected into a unified latent space as a point set characterized by structural offsets. Stage 3: A trainable interaction dynamics field computes all-directional velocity displacements via pairwise node-relation operations. Stage 4: Representations evolve across dozens of stacked non-dissipative interaction layers, avoiding over-smoothing while capturing deep multi-hop dependencies. Stage 5: Target metrics across node, relation, and whole-graph levels are directly read out from final evolved embeddings.}
  \label{fig:overall_framework}
\end{figure*}
